### Design Specificity Verdict

**The Verdict: Category-Interchangeable "Neo-Brutalism / Pastel Bento" Prompt Stack**

Balangay is conceptually framed as an offline-first Philippine personal finance companion named after the pre-colonial communal boat. However, its visual execution is a textbook collage of AI prompt tropes: *Soft Neo-Brutalism + Playful Pastel Bento + Dribbble Trend-Stacking*. If you swap the Peso sign (`₱`) for a Dollar (`$`) and remove the greeting word *"Kumusta"*, this interface is indistinguishable from a generic student habit logger, a pet tracker, or a crypto toy wallet.

- **LLM Assessment**: The design suffers from structural sameness. The entire architecture is forcibly carved into rounded Bento containers (`BentoCard.tsx`). The emotional tone is dissonant: real-world financial anxieties are trivialized by pastel fills (Butter `#FFED9E`, Blossom `#F2C0CA`, Pistachio `#DAE097`, Sky `#A6CFF2`), 4-point sparkle stars (`SparkleStar`), puffy hearts (`PuffyHeart`), and mechanically tilted sticker badges (`rotate-[-2deg]`). Furthermore, Philippine cultural identity is reduced to skin-deep nouns rather than authentic financial realities (e.g. semi-monthly 15th/30th budgeting rhythms, *petsa de peligro* cashflow buffers, family *padala*).
- **Deterministic Scan**:

  - Discovered 2 hard violations of Antislop R-02 (em dash `—` forbidden in UI text) in `index.html` title and description.

### Overall Impression

Balangay possesses an exceptional engineering foundation: pure zero-backend local-first storage, robust Dexie repositories, PBKDF2 PIN encryption, and strict monetary rounding. However, its UI layer is suffocating under generic AI aesthetics. By stripping away the pastel bento gimmicks and embracing an editorial, calm, and culturally grounded financial language, Balangay can transform from an AI tech demo into an indispensable, trustworthy financial instrument.

### Priority Issues

#### [P1] Issue 1: Overloaded Semantic Colors & Pastel Collage
- **What**: Butter (`#FFED9E`), Blossom (`#F2C0CA`), Pistachio (`#DAE097`), and Sky (`#A6CFF2`) are plastered decoratively across unrelated domains. Pistachio is used for income, offline status, budget health, and "peaceful" mood. Blossom is used for expenses, PIN locks, and categories. Butter is used for food, the primary floating action button (+ Add), and PWA install banners.
- **Why it matters**: Users cannot scan their money at a glance. Color ceases to communicate financial direction and instead produces sensory fatigue.
- **Fix**: Strip all decorative pastels from card surfaces. Adopt a calm, neutral canvas and strictly isolate color to financial semantics: crisp dark forest green (`#124224`) exclusively for positive inflow/savings, deep wine red (`#9E2A3B`) exclusively for negative outflow/expenses, and muted neutral tones for containers.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable colorize` / `$impeccable quieter`

#### [P1] Issue 2: AI Prompt Bento Grid & "Playful Sticker" Gimmicks
- **What**: Gratuitous Bento box framing across every screen, accompanied by `SparkleStar`, `Starburst`, `PuffyHeart`, and arbitrarily tilted `StickerBadge` components (`rotate-[-2deg]`, `rotate-[2deg]`).
- **Why it matters**: Trivializes financial management, signals low-effort AI generation, and creates awkward whitespace and cards-within-cards nesting.
- **Fix**: Delete `StickerIcons.tsx` and rotation props on badges. Replace the Bento grid with an editorial, content-driven layout: an authoritative financial summary header, a clean scannable transaction list, and structured progress meters without card wrappers.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable distill`

#### [P2] Issue 3: Visual Noise Floor & Cognitive Overload on Dashboard
- **What**: The dashboard has 5 competing visual blocks on a single screen, all with high-contrast borders and colored backgrounds, failing 6 of 8 cognitive load checks.
- **Why it matters**: The user does not know what to look at first; primary cashflow status is lost in the noise.
- **Fix**: Re-architect the dashboard into a clear 3-tier vertical hierarchy: (1) Total Net Worth & Monthly Net Inflow (unified calm header), (2) Category Budget Health progress (minimalist linear meters), (3) Recent Activity list with clear typography and zero decorative mood chips.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable layout`

#### [P2] Issue 4: Accessibility Deficits & Hard Gate Violations
- **What**: Em dash violations in `index.html:11, 18` (violating Antislop R-02); suppressed `overused-font` warning in `src/index.css:12`; low-contrast `stone-400` text on canvas (2.26:1 ratio, failing WCAG AA); non-semantic clickable `div`s lacking keyboard navigation (`App.tsx:75`, `DayInspector.tsx:140`); inputs with stripped focus outlines (`OnboardingModal.tsx:365`).
- **Why it matters**: Breaks screen reader operability, keyboard navigation, and basic WCAG compliance.
- **Fix**: Replace em dashes with colons/commas; replace `stone-400` with high-contrast text; convert clickable `div`s to semantic buttons or add `tabIndex={0}` and keyboard listeners; restore visible focus rings.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable audit` / `$impeccable harden`

#### [P3] Issue 5: Missing Query Loading States & App Error Resilience
- **What**: Cold-start queries in `BudgetsAnalyticsView`, `TransactionsView`, and `CashFlowView` default to `?? []`, causing flashes of empty states before IndexedDB emits records. No top-level `<ErrorBoundary>` in `App.tsx`.
- **Why it matters**: Degrades perceived performance and risks a complete white-screen crash if an unhandled IndexedDB or calculation error occurs.
- **Fix**: Add proper loading skeletons for initial database queries and wrap `src/App.tsx` in a resilient error boundary.
- **Suggested Command**: `$impeccable harden`
