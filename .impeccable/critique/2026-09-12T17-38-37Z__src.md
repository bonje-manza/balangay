#### Overall Impression

Balangay succeeds at creating a warm, friendly, and culturally authentic private finance tracker that rejects generic corporate fintech gloss. The local-first ethos is genuine. The primary design opportunity is moving beyond surface styling: introducing tactile micro-interactions (toasts, natural easing, horizontal shakes), establishing clear typographic and border hierarchy so static cards don't compete with interactive buttons, and ensuring mobile navigation retains immediate clarity.

#### What's Working

1. **Domestic Ergonomics & Voice**: Treating GCash, Maya, and cash as first-class accounts alongside banks speaks directly to how Filipinos actually budget, backed by warm and welcoming localization.
2. **Mindful Emotional Accounting**: The mood tags (*"Peaceful"*, *"Essential"*, *"Treat"*, *"Invest"*) provide a novel dimension to transaction classification that elevates the app from a bookkeeping tool to a mindful personal sanctuary.
3. **Local Sovereignty & Resilience**: 100% offline IndexedDB persistence with zero network telemetry, instant reactive balance calculation, and clean JSON/CSV export capabilities.

#### Priority Issues

##### 1. [P1] Silent Workflow Termination (Zero Post-Action Confirmation)
- **Why it matters**: Creating a transaction, transferring funds, or editing a budget dismisses the modal instantly with zero visual or tactile feedback. Users are left wondering if the data was committed or dropped (violating Heuristic 1).
- **Fix**: Implement an unobtrusive neo-brutalist toast notification (`<Toast message="₱350 recorded in GCash" variant="pistachio" action="Undo" />`) above the bottom nav dock for 3.5 seconds.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish` (or `$impeccable delight`)

##### 2. [P1] Irreversible Forgotten-PIN Lockout with Zero Recovery Gate
- **Why it matters**: Severe data-loss risk. PINs are salted and hashed locally with PBKDF2. If forgotten, `PinLockScreen` provides no reset or recovery hatch, permanently locking users out of their ledger.
- **Fix**: Add an explicit setup warning (*"Balangay is 100% offline. If forgotten, your vault can only be restored using a JSON backup file."*) and an emergency fallback on `PinLockScreen` (*"Forgot PIN? Restore from Backup JSON"*).
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

##### 3. [P2] Vertical `animate-bounce` on PIN Error Instead of Horizontal Shake
- **Why it matters**: When an incorrect PIN is entered, `isShaking` triggers Tailwind's `animate-bounce`. Up-and-down hopping feels cartoonish and contradicts standard security failure patterns (like iOS/Android horizontal head-shake).
- **Fix**: Replace `animate-bounce` with a smooth horizontal shake keyframe animation with exponential deceleration (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`).
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable animate`

##### 4. [P2] Mobile Navigation Degradation to "Mystery Meat" Icons
- **Why it matters**: On viewports `< 640px`, `FloatingNavBar` hides all text labels (`hidden sm:inline`), showing only 5 black icons. Distracted or first-time mobile users cannot reliably distinguish between "Ledger" and "Wallets" without labels.
- **Fix**: Keep compact 10px labels beneath icons on mobile or shorten labels (*"Home"*, *"Ledger"*, *"Budgets"*, *"Vault"*, *"Settings"*) so text is never dropped.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

##### 5. [P2] Visual Noise Ceiling & Border Homogeneity (Tactile Fatigue)
- **Why it matters**: Universal 2px black borders and hard offset shadows on cards, sub-cards, inputs, badges, and buttons flatten the visual hierarchy and tire the eyes during extended reviews.
- **Fix**: Reserve 2px borders and tactile shadows for interactive elements (buttons, active pills, modal windows); soften internal static bento cards and list rows to hairline `border-stone-800/15` without drop shadows.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable quieter` (or `$impeccable layout`)

##### 6. [P2] Monolithic Transaction Form Lacks Progressive Disclosure
- **Why it matters**: `TransactionFormModal` displays 8 fields simultaneously (Type, Amount, Account, Category, Date, Notes, Tags, Mood), creating friction for quick on-the-go logging.
- **Fix**: Group Amount, Account, and Category prominently at the top; collapse Date, Notes, Tags, and Mood under an expandable *"Add details, mood, or tags +"* drawer.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`
